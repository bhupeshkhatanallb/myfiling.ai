Security

Your documents, protected

Filing documents are sensitive. We designed myfiling.ai to handle them with care at every step - from upload to report.

Authentication

Accounts are protected with hashed passwords and server-side sessions. Session cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite, and are sent over HTTPS in production so credentials are never exposed to client-side scripts.

We never save your PDF

Your uploaded PDF is analysed in memory only and deleted the moment your report is produced - it is never written to permanent storage, never shared, and never used to train models. Only the resulting report is kept, scoped to your account.

User privacy

Your reports and history are scoped to your account. We don't sell your data, and we don't use your filings to train models. What you upload stays yours.

Secure access

Every analysis and history endpoint requires an authenticated session. Logging out ends the session immediately, and access is always checked on the server - never assumed from the client.

Reporting a concern

If you believe you've found a security issue, please contact us with the details. We take reports seriously and will respond as quickly as we can.

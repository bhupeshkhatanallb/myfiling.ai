Our mission

We make filing readiness measurable

Filing should never fail on a technicality. myfiling.ai turns the registry's expectations into an instant, objective check - so legal teams file with confidence the first time.

Why we built it

Every filing team knows the cost of a rejection: a wasted trip to the registry, a queue, and a deadline that just got tighter. The reasons are almost always avoidable - a margin a few millimetres short, a missing endorsement, an index that doesn't reconcile. The expertise to catch these exists; what's missing is a consistent, fast way to apply it to every document, every time.

myfiling.ai encodes court filing requirements into an analysis engine that reads a PDF the way a careful scrutiny clerk would - measuring paper size, margins, type face and size, line spacing, document structure, and the presence of required documents - and reports back in seconds with evidence behind every finding.

What the product does

You upload a complete filing PDF. The engine produces a filing-readiness score and a prioritised defect checklist: critical items that would bounce at the counter, warnings the office is likely to object to, and advisory notes. Each finding cites the page and the measurement behind it, so there's nothing to argue with - only something to fix.

Reports are saved to your account so you can revisit any past analysis, and a court rules reference keeps the requirements behind each check close at hand.

Our principles

  • Evidence over opinion. Every flag is backed by a measurement and a page reference.
  • Precision over noise. The score reflects real objection risk, not raw defect count, so cosmetic items never overshadow what matters.
  • Privacy by default. Documents are processed for analysis and not retained; your reports are yours alone.

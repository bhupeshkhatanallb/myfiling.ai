Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how myfiling.ai ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and application (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address, a securely hashed password, and, optionally, your name. We use this information to identify you, secure your account, and provide access to your reports and history.

Documents you upload

When you analyse a filing, you upload a PDF document. We process this document to generate your filing-readiness report. We do not retain the uploaded PDF after the report is generated; the temporary copy used for processing is deleted once analysis completes.

Analysis results

We store the results of each analysis - the readiness score, the defect findings, and related metadata such as the file name, document size, court selection, and timestamp - and associate them with your account so you can review past reports.

Technical information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate the Service, such as server logs containing request metadata. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure;
  • To generate, store, and display your filing-readiness reports and history;
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support;
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or misuse.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your uploaded documents to train machine-learning models.

3. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process your information to perform our contract with you (providing the Service), to pursue our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service, and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Data retention

Uploaded PDFs are not retained after processing. Account information and analysis results are retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Data sharing

We share your information only with service providers who help us operate the Service (for example, hosting infrastructure), and only to the extent necessary. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of users and the public.

6. Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including password hashing, server-side sessions, and HTTPS in production. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and review our practices regularly. See our Security page for more detail.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us.

8. Cookies

We use a small number of strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to operate the Service. See our Cookie Policy for details.

9. Children

The Service is intended for legal and professional use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated through the Service.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.

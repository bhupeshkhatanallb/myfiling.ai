Cookie Policy

Last updated: 29 June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how myfiling.ai uses cookies and similar technologies. We keep our use of cookies to the minimum needed to operate the Service.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They allow a website to remember information between requests - for example, that you are signed in.

2. Cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in after you log in or create an account. This cookie is essential to provide access to your dashboard, reports, and history. It is set as HttpOnly (not accessible to client-side scripts) and SameSite, and is transmitted securely over HTTPS in production. Without it, you would have to authenticate on every request.

Local browser storage

When you start an analysis from the homepage before signing in, your browser may temporarily store the details of the file you selected (in session storage) so that, after you log in, your analysis can continue without re-selecting the file. This data stays on your device, is scoped to the current browser session, and is cleared once it is used or the session ends.

3. Cookies we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or third-party marketing trackers.

4. Managing cookies

Most browsers let you view, manage, and delete cookies through their settings. Because our session cookie is strictly necessary, disabling it will prevent you from staying signed in and using the application.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as the Service evolves. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

6. Contact

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us. See also our Privacy Policy.

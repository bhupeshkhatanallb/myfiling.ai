The problem

Filing rejections cost time the case can't afford

A single overlooked detail can send a filing back from the registry. The common culprits are predictable - and preventable.

Registry objections

Papers bounce at the counter over defects that a checklist would have caught in minutes.

Formatting defects

Wrong margins, type size, line spacing or paper size - small misses, repeated objections.

Missing documents

An index, vakalatnama, affidavit or certified copy left out stalls the whole filing.

Filing delays

Each rejection means another trip, another queue, and a deadline creeping closer.
